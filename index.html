Pincher Scrap (Gripping Allowance) — خردة الكلابة

mm Applied ONLY to sections from the smallest up to L90 (inclusive). This length is reserved on each raw bar for the pincher grip, so the punch never hits the pincher. Larger sections (L100 and above) are not affected.

Example: pieces total = 11,000 mm, Pincher Scrap = 100 mm → consumed = 11,100 mm. On a 12,000 mm bar the remaining scrap = 12,000 − 11,100 = 900 mm.
